Star class codes

Every system shows a star class like G7pf on the galaxy map. The letter is what matters in game: it sets the star's colour, which metal its planets have and which hyperdrive you need. The number and the small letters come from real astronomy and are only flavour in No Man's Sky.

The number (0–9)

How hot the star is within its letter: 0 is the hottest, 9 the coolest. A G2 is a little hotter than a G7. It doesn't change anything in game.

Portal address

Numbered in the order the portal's own keypad shows them, 1 to 16. On a keyboard, type the hex code (0–9, A–F); Backspace removes, arrow keys move between spots.

Same system, other planet

The first glyph is the planet number inside the system. The other eleven are the system's location, so every planet in a system shares them. Waterworlds often have no portal, and dialing one drops you on the seafloor.

Species discovered per planet

How many creatures, plants and minerals you've scanned on each planet, from your save. Your save doesn't know how many a planet has in total, so use this as a shortlist: planets with lots of scans are likely complete. Your save

Your quest lines and goals, filled in from where you're at. Change the status or notes as you go; it's remembered in this browser.

Save tools are for testing, restoring and fixing saves. Every change waits in the list below until you press Write to save. The app refuses to write while No Man's Sky is running, backs the save up first and checks the result afterwards.

Everything you've starred, in one place.

Recipes and item values come from the game files (version 7.00) via bradhave94/nms. Values are base prices; real prices shift with each system's economy.

    Recipes
    Pick Refiner, Crafting or Cooking, then search any item. Uses it shows recipes that need the item, Makes it shows recipes that produce it. Searching an exact item name shows an info card. Click any item chip to jump to it. ☆ pins a recipe to the Pinned tab.
    The × number is output value ÷ input value, so above ×1 makes you money. Profit / run is units gained each run. Items in → out ignores value and just counts items.
    
    
    Recipes you can make now
    In Recipes, tick Only recipes I can make now. The list then only shows recipes you have the materials for, with how many times you can run each one, and the counts appear on the Refiner, Crafting and Cooking buttons. Search still works on top of it.
    Choose From my save and tick which inventories count (exosuit, ships, corvettes, freighter, base storage, exocraft), or My own list to type items and amounts in yourself.
    
    
    How many can I make
    Every recipe shows three counts worked out from what you have: now (only the ingredients you already carry), with sub-crafts (also making missing ingredients from what you have, one step down) and from scratch (all the way down to raw materials). Materials shared between ingredients are only counted once. Click the counts for the full ingredient tree, where each ingredient is stored, and exactly what you're short to make one more. Untick Show how many I can make to hide them.
    
    
    Where to find
    A star class guide (type a code like G7pf to decode it), tables for star colours and planet types, plus the game's own description for every raw material. Switch to Fish to search by biome.
    
    
    Item icons
    In the desktop app, the real item icons are copied out of your own No Man's Sky install the first time it runs. It takes a few minutes in the background, and you can watch it in Settings & help, Game files. They're kept in the app's data folder and refreshed by themselves after a game update. Nothing in your game folder is changed, and no game artwork comes with the app.
    
    
    Inventory
    Your exosuit, starships, corvettes (their own inventories plus the storage units built into them), freighter and base storage containers, read from your save. Everything adds up each item across all of them and shows where it is. Click an item to see its recipes. It refreshes whenever the app syncs.
    In Everything, the filters narrow the list by kind (raw, refined, basic or advanced crafted, trade goods, curiosities, food, fish, tech, base parts, corvette parts) and by which inventories count, including corvette workshop storage. Hide corvette parts, Only things used in recipes and Worth selling work on top. Your filters are remembered; Clear filters resets them.
    In Recipes, tick Only recipes I can make now to see what you can make with what you actually have.
    
    
    Portals
    Tap glyphs on the keypad (numbered 1–16 like the portal) or paste a code. Each glyph pops into the address bar, which stays in view while you scroll. Tap any of the 12 spots to pick it: with Replace the next glyph overwrites that spot, with Insert it goes in before it and the rest move along. The × on a spot removes that glyph. Undo steps back through your changes. On a keyboard, type the hex code (0–9, A–F).
    The first glyph is the planet, and the planet buttons swap it to reach other planets in the same system. Save addresses with a name; Copy puts the numbers on your clipboard.
    
    
    My galaxy
    Drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom; arrow keys work too. Pick a camera: Top down, Angled, Side or Front, or turn it freely with the sliders, Right-drag or Shift-drag (Q and E turn it with the keyboard). Height stretches the galaxy's thickness so you can see which systems sit above or below the plane. The Galaxy list switches between the galaxies your save has data for.
    Click a system for details, then Open system view for its planets. Switch between colouring by type and by who discovered it.
    
    
    Sync
    In the desktop app, Sync reads your newest save. Tick Auto-sync next to the Sync button at the top, and every tab keeps itself up to date while you play: inventory, recipes you can make, the map and your stats. Pick how often it checks in the Sync section of this menu. The game only writes a new save when you land, get out of your ship, warp or save manually, so changes show up after that.
    
    
    Save tools (advanced)
    Off by default. Turn them on in Settings & help, Game files, Advanced. A Save tools tab appears with: Saves & backups (back up any save, restore a backup, or make an older save the one the game loads), Inventories (any item and amount in any slot, unlock slots), Currencies, Settlements (finish construction, debt, name, stats, buildings, perks, production, timers, or the whole entry raw), Quests (change a step, remove a mission, or restore missions from a backup), Timers (move any date in the save back, like the clock trick) and Everything (raw) to browse and change any value.
    Changes wait in a list until you press Write to save. The app refuses to write while No Man's Sky is running, backs the save up to data\backups first, then reads it back to check it. If anything looks wrong in game, restore the backup.
    
    
    Reporting a bug
    Open Report a bug in this menu, say what happened and how, and save, copy or download the report. The attached log has no save data, names, locations or personal details; you can read all of it before sending.
    
    
    Quests
    Change a quest's status with its dropdown and write your next step in the notes. Add a goal makes your own card; double-click a title to rename it.
    
    
    Keyboard shortcuts
    Ctrl+K search everything · 1–8 switch tabs · / jump to the search box · G open or close Goals · ? this help · Esc close a panel.
    
    
    Goals
    Press Goals at the top (or G) for a side panel with your pinned recipes and your own goals. Make a goal, then add rows: an item and how many, money, a recipe to be able to make, or a quest to finish. Every row updates by itself from your save, ticks off when done, and the panel lists what you still need to gather. Drag goals to reorder them.
    
    
    Display
    Settings → Display picks how portal glyphs show (icons, numbers, names, hex), whether big numbers are written in full or short (1.37B), how a system's location is given, and whether inventories show as a grid like in the game or as a list.
    
    
    Item cards
    Search an item by its exact name to get its card. You own adds it up across all your inventories; click it to see where it's stored. Where to find (or double-click the card) jumps to where it's gathered, or to the recipes that make it.
    
    
    Where is my stuff saved?
    Pins, storage, saved addresses, notes and these settings are kept in this browser only. They won't follow you to another device, and clearing your browser data removes them.
