NMS Save Tracker 2.4.3 (beta)
An unofficial, fan-made companion app for No Man's Sky on PC (Steam/GOG). Not made by or affiliated with Hello Games.

WHAT IT DOES
- Reads your No Man's Sky save (read only) and shows your inventories, what you can craft or refine,
  where to find resources and trade goods, your galaxy map, portal addresses and quests.
- Goals: track items, money, recipes or quests; they tick themselves off as you play.
- In-game overlay: F8 opens a small window on top of the game with your goals and what you still need
  (click an item to see how to get it). F9 closes it. F10 shows the whole app as a side panel.
  For the overlay, set No Man's Sky to Borderless (Options > Video > Window mode).
- Save tools (advanced, off by default): edit your save, always with a backup first.

FIRST RUN
- Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the app isn't code-signed.
  Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
- The app finds your saves and game folder by itself. Item icons are read from your own game files
  the first time (takes a minute).
- Closing the window keeps the app in the tray (bottom right) so the hotkeys work.
  Right-click the tray icon and choose Quit to close it fully.

VISIT US
Portals tab > saved addresses: "Bountria Prime - Verdantia", the capital of the Bountria Federation.
Load it on the glyph pad and dial it at any portal.

YOUR DATA
Everything stays on your PC, in the "data" folder inside the install folder.
Uninstalling asks whether to keep it.
